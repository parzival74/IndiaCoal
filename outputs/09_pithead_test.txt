READ (honest):
==========================================================================
- The pithead flag is a curated PROXY but is independently validated by the
  real CERC ECR ladder (cross-check 2): pithead stations are the cheapest.
- Cost-side test (2b) is the true test of the claim, but rests on 14 CERC
  stations on a 2018-19 basis -- DIRECTIONAL, not a settled result. It
  becomes answerable once FY2022-23 per-station ECR coverage is broad.
- PLF-side (2a) is muddied by lignite: cheap mine-mouth lignite runs hard
  DESPITE low efficiency, which is itself the cost>efficiency point.
- Step 3 shows whether any pithead tightening survives the sector/size
  confounder; see the interaction term and ΔR² above.
