Fleet: 455 units | total net generation held at 763,316 GWh
Per-unit annual ceiling: 85% PLF | VC & EF held fixed

Scenario                 Fuel cost (Rs cr)      CO2 (MT)    Served (GWh)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Actual (as-run)                 188,599         777.9         763,316
2. Cost-merit                      180,252         764.9         763,316
3. Carbon-merit                    194,808         746.0         763,316

Deltas vs actual:
  Cost-merit   : fuel cost -8,347 cr (-4.4%),  CO2 -12.9 MT (-1.7%)
  Carbon-merit : fuel cost +6,209 cr (+3.3%),  CO2 -31.9 MT (-4.1%)

The trade-off, isolated (cost-merit minus carbon-merit):
  Chasing least COST instead of least CARBON costs +18.9 MT of extra CO2 while saving +14,556 cr.

INTERPRETATION: minimum-cost and minimum-carbon dispatch are NOT the
same objective for this fleet. The cheapest units (pithead lignite/
domestic) are among the dirtiest; the cleanest (imported) are dearest.
So 'follow strict merit order' saves money but does not minimise CO2 --
the two goals require a carbon price to reconcile. (Magnitudes scale
with the fuel-price assumptions in 02_variable_cost.py.)
